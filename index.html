About Me

My journey into data analysis started with something simple: curiosity. I’ve always been fascinated by data and enjoyed learning more about it, but I didn’t know where this interest could take me until I discovered Data Analysis. The moment I learned more about the field, I realized that it was more than just working with numbers — it was about turning data into insights, finding patterns, and using information to make better decisions. That curiosity quickly turned into a passion, and I decided to take my first step into the field by joining the DEPI initiative, where I began developing my skills and exploring the world of data analysis. Since then, I’ve been continuously learning, practicing, and building my skills in Excel, Power BI, and data analysis. And this is only the beginning of my journey. I’m excited to keep learning, take on new challenges, and turn data into stories that create real value.
